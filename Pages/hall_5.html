STARK INDUSTRIES ARCHIVE

THE LEGACY

WHAT REMAINS AFTER EVERYTHING

WHAT HE BUILT

Stark Industries. The Iron Legion. The Avengers Initiative. Clean energy research. Time travel. He didn't just solve problems. He built systems that outlasted him. The work continues because he made sure it would.

WHAT HE CHANGED

He proved that one person with enough determination can alter the course of history. Not because he was perfect. Because he refused to be irrelevant. Every person who watched him stand up one more time learned something about what's possible.

WHAT HE LEFT BEHIND

A daughter. A legacy. A standard that didn't exist before him. He left behind proof that genius means nothing without conscience, that power means nothing without responsibility, and that the greatest thing a person can build is something worth protecting.

Every legacy leaves a question.

Who carries it forward?
