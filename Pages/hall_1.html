TONY STARK

Chief Executive Officer

Anthony Edward Stark was an inventor, engineer, billionaire, and the founder of Iron Man.

THE GENIUS

Anthony Edward Stark. Born May 29, 1970. CEO of Stark Industries. Physicist. Engineer. Futurist. A man whose genius and relentless curiosity drove him to question everything and accept impossibility as a challenge.

THE MAN

Sarcastic by default. Confident to a fault. Beneath the armor of wit was a man who felt everything deeply and showed it to almost no one. His experiences forced him to confront his mistakes, his weapons, and the consequences of his choices.

THE LEGACY

What ultimately defined Stark was not intelligence, but accountability. He chose responsibility over comfort and sacrifice over survival. He made mistakes at a scale few could imagine — and chose to own every one of them.
