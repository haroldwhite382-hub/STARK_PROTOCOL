SUBJECT: ANTHONY EDWARD STARK

Anthony Edward Stark. Born May 29, 1970. CEO of Stark Industries. Physicist. Engineer. Futurist. The most intelligent man in any room he has ever entered.

Stark operated on genius-level intellect paired with relentless curiosity. Sarcastic by default. Confident to a fault. He never stopped asking questions and never accepted impossibility as a final answer. Underneath the armor of wit was a man who felt everything deeply and showed it to almost no one.

He built weapons. He believed in them. Then one moment — one single experience — forced him to see exactly what those weapons had become. He did not look away. He did not make excuses. He went back to the beginning and rebuilt everything. Including himself.

What defined Stark was not intelligence. It was accountability. He made mistakes at a scale most people cannot imagine — and he owned every single one. He chose responsibility when he could have chosen comfort. He chose sacrifice when he could have chosen survival.

"Part of the journey is the end."
