ROBERT DOWNEY JR

Actor

Robert John Downey Jr. is the actor who gave Tony Stark his wit, his swagger, and his heart.

THE ACTOR

Born April 4, 1965, in New York City. He grew up around film sets and became one of Hollywood's most gifted performers, earning an Oscar nomination for Chaplin in 1992. Sharp, quick, and impossible to ignore.

THE COMEBACK

Success did not come easily. Struggles with addiction cost him years, roles, and nearly his career. He rebuilt himself slowly, one honest step at a time, until Hollywood was willing to believe in him again. In 2008, that chance was Iron Man.

THE ICON

He did not just play Tony Stark. He launched the Marvel Cinematic Universe with Iron Man in 2008 and carried it through Avengers: Endgame in 2019. Years later, he won an Academy Award for Oppenheimer. Like Tony, he knew what it was to fall, and what it took to rebuild.
