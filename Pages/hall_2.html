SUBJECT: Robert Downey Jr

Robert John Downey Jr. Born April 4, 1965. Actor. Producer. One of the most talented and most troubled performers of his generation. A man who fell completely — and came back stronger than anyone expected.

Through the 1990s, Downey's career collapsed under the weight of addiction and legal trouble. He was written off. Dismissed. Considered a liability by an industry that had once celebrated him. Most people assumed his story was over.

He proved them wrong. Through discipline, support, and sheer determination, Downey rebuilt his life and his career — one role at a time. By 2008, he was ready for the role that would define a generation.

When Marvel cast Downey as Tony Stark, most considered it a risk. It became the greatest casting decision in superhero history. He did not play Stark. He understood him. Because in every way that mattered, he already was him.

"Do I need to be Iron Man? No. I am Iron Man."
